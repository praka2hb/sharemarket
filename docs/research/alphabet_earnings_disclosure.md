# What Alphabet discloses in its earnings release

* **Date:** 2026-11-15
* **Source:** Alphabet Q3 2026 earnings release (Exhibit 99.1, 9 pages, `s206.q4cdn.com/.../2026q3-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf`), cross-checked with press coverage (Yahoo/Zacks, AlphaSense, The Desk). Figures in USD millions unless stated.
* **Why:** to see which parts of Alphabet are reported separately, and could be split into tradable pieces or shown as reference numbers in the app.

**Cloud's share of segment profit** (Cloud ÷ (Services + Cloud) operating income): **22.1%**, up from 9.7% a year earlier. That's close to its 24.2% revenue share now; a year ago profit lagged far behind revenue.

## 3. Everything else in the release

* **Headline items:** revenue growth (reported and constant currency), operating margin, net income, diluted EPS ($2.12, down from $2.87 because of an $8.7B loss on equity securities).
* **Traffic acquisition costs (TAC):** $16,366M.
* **Employees:** 204,757.
* **Revenue by geography:** United States, EMEA, APAC, Other Americas. The US was 53% of Q3 2026 revenue.
* **Full income statement:** cost of revenues, R&D, sales and marketing, G&A, other income, taxes.
* **Cash flow:**
  * operating cash flow $42,290M;
  * capex (purchases of property and equipment) $51,355M;
  * **free cash flow −$9,065M** (negative for two quarters running).
* **Balance sheet:** total assets passed $1 trillion. Cash and securities $265B, long-term debt $125B.
* **Shareholder returns:** dividends $2,936M; no buybacks this quarter. It issued $12.4B of new common stock and $29B of debt.

## 4. Said on the call or in the slides, not in the release tables

* **Google Cloud backlog:** about $561B; just over 55% expected as revenue within 24 months.
* **Gemini app:** over 1 billion monthly users. First-party model APIs process about 32 billion tokens a minute.
* **YouTube Music and Premium:** 150M+ subscribers, including trials.
* **TPU system sales:** a "meaningful contributor" to Cloud, but no separate figure.
* **2026 capex guidance:** $195–205B.
* **Q4 outlook:** a larger foreign-exchange headwind, mainly in Search and YouTube ads.

## 5. What Alphabet does *not* break out

* **Total YouTube revenue.** YouTube ads is its own line, but YouTube subscriptions are mixed into "subscriptions, platforms and devices" along with Google One, Play and Pixel.
* **Waymo** on its own. It sits inside Other Bets.
* **Gemini or AI revenue** as a number.
* **Google Cloud Platform vs Workspace,** and **TPU sales** within Cloud.
* **Operating income below segment level:** no profit figure for Search or YouTube alone.

## 6. What this means for us

1. **Only two pieces have both revenue and profit reported: Services and Cloud.** Our `$CLOUD` / `$SERVICES` split is the one split Alphabet fully supports with official numbers every quarter.
2. **Finer splits are possible on revenue only.** Search, YouTube ads, Network, subscriptions/devices and Cloud are each reported quarterly, so a five-way split (`$SEARCH`, `$YOUTUBE`, `$CLOUD`, ...) could show a reported share for each, but no profit share.
3. **Which number to show next to the market price.** With settlement dropped, it's information only. The app could show two:
   * **revenue share:** Cloud 24.2%;
   * **profit share:** Cloud 22.1%.

   Profit share is arguably closer to how investors value a business, and Cloud's has more than doubled in a year. Showing both answers "what is `$CLOUD` actually tracking?" honestly.
4. **The story judges will get is strong and current:**
   * Cloud doubled in a year;
   * it now earns 22% of Alphabet's segment profit, up from 10%;
   * it has a $561B backlog;
   * Alphabet is borrowing and issuing stock to fund AI capex, with negative free cash flow.

   "Do you believe in the Cloud/AI bet or in Search?" is a live debate, which is exactly what `$CLOUD` vs `$SERVICES` lets people take a side on.
5. **Things a piece can't capture:**
   * Alphabet-level costs (−$7.7B) and Other Bets (−$2.0B) fall outside both pieces;
   * so do the equity-securities gains and losses that swung EPS.

   `$CLOUD` + `$SERVICES` = 1 GOOGLc still includes all of them, because the pair is the whole stock.
