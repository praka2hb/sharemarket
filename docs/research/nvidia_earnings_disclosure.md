## 1. The big change: Nvidia now reports fewer lines

Until fiscal 2026 Nvidia reported five market platforms: Data Center (split into compute and networking), Gaming, Professional Visualization, Automotive, and OEM & Other.

**From fiscal 2027 (Q1 onwards) it reports two platforms:**

* **Data Center**, split by *customer type*, not by product:
  * **Hyperscale:** the largest cloud providers.
  * **AI Clouds, Industrial & Enterprise (ACIE):** neoclouds, sovereign AI, enterprises. Derived as Data Center − Hyperscale.
* **Edge Computing:** the old Gaming + Pro Visualization + Automotive + OEM & Other, merged into one line.

**Gaming, Automotive and the compute vs networking split are no longer reported as numbers.** Management still mentions networking growth on the call (for example "networking revenue up 18% sequentially") but gives no figure in the tables.

## 4. Everything else in the release

* **Headline figures:** revenue $96.2B (+106% year on year), gross margin 75.0%, operating income $63.7B, net income $59.7B, diluted EPS $2.46 (non-GAAP $2.22).
* **Guidance:** Q3 FY2027 revenue of $108.0B ± 2%, with no China data-center compute assumed.
* **Cash flow:** operating cash flow $24.1B, capex $2.7B, free cash flow $21.3B.
* **Shareholder returns:** $19.7B of buybacks and $6.0B of dividends. It also issued $25B of notes.
* **Revenue by customer location,** and customers above 10% of revenue.
* **From the call:**
  * Vera Rubin is shipping and expected to be about 20% of Q3 data-center revenue;
  * AWS is adding 2M GPUs;
  * neoclouds should reach about 8 GW of installed capacity by year end.
